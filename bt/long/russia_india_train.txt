Bhai… socho ek baar. Moscow se ek train nikalti hai. Aur seedha… India pahunchti hai.

Nahi, yeh koi film nahi hai. Nahi, yeh koi WhatsApp forward nahi hai. Yeh baat Russia ke Deputy Prime Minister ne kahi hai.

Matlab Moscow ab seriously soch raha hai — India aur Russia ko ek railway line se joda jaaye.

Ab sawaal yeh nahi ki train kitni tez chalegi. Sawaal yeh hai… Kyun? Kyun Russia itna paagalpan dikha raha hai India tak train laane ke liye? Kyun centuries se unka dil Hind Mahasagar pe atka pada hai?

Aaj main aapko woh kahani sunata hoon… jo sirf maps pe nahi, history pe likhi hui hai. Aur last mein aapse ek seedha sawaal poochunga — India ko is plan pe sochna chahiye… ya bilkul door rehna chahiye?

Pehle ek simple baat samajh lo. Aaj duniya ka lagbhag forty to fifty percent global trade Hind Mahasagar se hota hai. Oil ki baat karo… toh duniya ke bade oil routes ka bada hissa isi ocean ke bina possible hi nahi.

Ab socho — Russia duniya ka sabse bada desh hai size mein. Par ek problem hai. Unke paas warm water ports ka woh easy, direct access nahi… jo Hind Mahasagar deta hai.

Aur yeh naya sapna nahi hai. Nineteen eighties mein ek famous article aaya tha — The Great Game in Asia. Usme ek line aisi thi jo aaj bhi kaam karti hai: Russia hamesha yeh maanta raha… ki Hind Mahasagar tak pahunchna unki historic destiny hai. Unki taqdeer mein likha hai.

Britain ne unhe rokne ki koshish ki. Afghanistan mein. Iran ke around treaties mein. Baar-baar. Matlab yeh sirf trade nahi. Yeh power ka game hai. Aur power ka game… kabhi budha nahi hota.

Aaj agar koi cargo ship Hind Mahasagar se Russia jaaye, toh raaste mushkil hain. Ek raasta — Bab-el-Mandeb se. Yemen ke rebels, attacks… area unsafe ban chuka hai. Hormuz jaisa tension… yahan bhi.

Dusra lamba raasta — Malacca Strait, South China Sea, phir Bering… bahut door, bahut mehnga, bahut time.

Aur Russia ki asli population? Eighty to ninety percent log west side pe hain — Moscow, St. Petersburg. East side density ke hisaab se khali-sa hai. Toh industry ko chahiye short, safe, reliable route. Samundar ab pehle jaisa easy nahi raha. Isliye ab nazar train pe hai. Road pe hai. Land corridor pe hai. Jab sea risky ho… toh zameen pe raasta nikalna padta hai.

Ab suno asli plan. Russia ka idea roughly aisa hai: Russia se train… Central Asia se hote hue… Turkmenistan… Iran… Afghanistan… Pakistan… aur phir India. Kabhi Caspian Sea ke through cargo bhi move ho sakta hai, phir wahan se train network se aage.

Russia ka clear message hai: Jo bhi option India tak pahuncha de… woh acceptable hai.

Ab yahan ek baat dhyaan se sunna. Yeh Chabahar port wala North-South Corridor jaisa exact same nahi hai. Yeh alag soch hai — zyada seedha railway dream. Aur haan, yeh proposal abhi officially India government ke paas final deal ke roop mein nahi aaya… lekin Russia desperate zaroor dikhta hai.

Kyun? Kyunki agar yeh train ban gayi… toh oil trade, defence equipment, goods… sab land pe move kar sakta hai. Sanctions ke duniya mein… yeh game-changer ho sakta hai Russia ke liye. Aur India ke liye? Yeh opportunity bhi ho sakti hai… aur badi pechiida pechaan bhi.

Ab meri baat India se. Hum hamesha yeh sunte aaye hain — India ki location iski sabse badi taakat hai. Hum Hind Mahasagar ke dil mein baithe hain. Agar koi badi taakat humse connect hona chahti hai… toh matlab hum sirf market nahi hain. Hum bridge hain. Hum gateway hain. Yeh baat pride ki hai.

Lekin pride ke saath akal bhi chahiye. Kyunki jo route Russia soch raha hai… usme Afghanistan hai. Pakistan hai.

Ab socho: Train Pakistan se hoke India aaye. Transit fee? Security? Agar kal koi attack, bandish, ya political blackmail ho gaya… toh poora corridor hostage ban sakta hai.

Kuch log kehte hain — jab PoK wapas mile, tab seedha northern route socha jaaye. Sach hai, woh geography alag khul sakti hai. Lekin woh aaj ka reality nahi. Woh future ka chapter hai. Mountains, phases, time… sab lagega.

Toh aaj ka sawaal emotional nahi, practical hai: India apni strength dikhaye… ya apni vulnerability badhaye? Kyunki Great Game pehle Britain aur Russia ke beech tha. Aaj nayi duniya mein… US-UK jaise players bhi nahi chahenge ki Russia is connectivity race mein aaram se jeet jaaye. Yeh sirf rail line nahi. Yeh influence ki line hai.

Is saal BRICS summit ke around bade leaders India aayenge. Connectivity ke proposals aane wale hain — roads, corridors, trains… sab discussion mein rahega. Duniya ab wapas land routes pe soch rahi hai. Kyunki samundar ke choke points pe tension badh gaya hai.

Toh ab aapse seedha sawaal: Agar yeh Russia–India train ka idea aage badhe, aur raasta Iran–Afghanistan–Pakistan se hoke aaye — jahan transit fee bhi deni pade aur security risk bhi ho — kya India ko is plan pe seriously sochna chahiye?

Ya aapka kehna hai: Bhai, jo raasta Pakistan se jaaye… usme mat uljhna. Better safe corridors pe focus karo.

Apna clear opinion comment mein likho — HAAN sochna chahiye… ya NAHI, door rehna chahiye.

Kyunki yeh train agar kabhi bani… toh yeh sirf bogie nahi laayegi. Yeh history, trade, aur power ka naya chapter laayegi. Aur India us chapter ka passenger nahi… driver banna chahti hai.

Bas itna yaad rakhna: Geography change nahi hoti. Lekin jo desh geography ko smartly use karta hai… wahi history likhta hai.
